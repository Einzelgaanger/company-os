Single source of truth for public copy. All page drafts draw from here.

## 6.1 Positioning

1. **Competitive alternatives:** Slack/Telegram threads; spreadsheets; manual PM chase; meeting-note tools without follow-through; task boards (Asana/Linear/Notion) that wait for people to open them.
2. **Unique capabilities (Shipped):** Commitment ownership (C1); extract + review (C2, C3); in-app Chat + preferred channel (C4, C5); Telegram (C6); scheduled check-ins (C8); context escalation + sensitivity (C9, C10); leadership reports (C11); Waiting/Flow (C12); coordination modes (C19).
3. **Differentiated value:** Waiting becomes visible; follow-through happens between meetings; stuck work reaches the right person with context; leads get operational reports without building a scorecard.
4. **Best-fit customers:** Studios, agencies and founding teams (5–80 people) where work lives in chat and meetings; ops/project leads who refuse another dashboard; multi-thread principals who need a short decision queue. Trigger: missed handoffs, Friday fiction reports, chase culture.
5. **Market category:** **"Agentic chief of staff" for work follow-through** (subsegment of work coordination / operating system for commitments). Existing "project management" frame is wrong (pull boards). New pure category would over-explain. Chief of staff + follow-through lands the job.
6. **Why now:** Distributed chat-first work; AI can extract and draft but humans still need governed loops; buyers distrust "AI-powered" dashboards that become HR tools.

## 6.2 Narrative spine

1. **Shift:** Promises now live in meetings and chat, not in a single system of record.
2. **Stakes:** Invisible waiting burns calendar, trust and delivery dates.
3. **Old way breaks:** Threads go quiet. Spreadsheets rot. Boards wait for people to open them. Meeting notes evaporate.
4. **New way:** Make every commitment owned, check in where people already work (Chat or messaging), escalate stuck work with context, report the operating picture.
5. **Proof:** Six-beat loop in product: detect, track, check, nudge, escalate, report (C1–C12).

Customer is the hero (PM, operator, person doing the work). Product is the guide.

## 6.4 Messaging hierarchy

### Product definition (2 sentences)
Company OS is an agentic chief of staff for teams that lose commitments in meetings and chat. It captures owned commitments, checks in via Chat or each person's messaging channel, escalates blockers with context and gives project leads operational reports (not people scorecards).

### One-liner (<12 words)
Keeps team promises alive between meetings.

### Hero headline candidates
1. Outcome: "Stop chasing. Close the work." 
2. Category + audience: "Chief of staff software for project follow-through"
3. Problem: "Waiting is invisible. Company OS makes it owned."
4. Mechanism: "Commitments, check-ins and context-aware unblocks"
5. Audience: "For operators who refuse chase culture"

**Pick:** Keep brand as H1 (`Company OS`) and slogan as category line. Sharpen **promise** (subhead) to the product definition. Do not replace brand-first hero (design rule).

### Hero subheadline (promise)
Captures owned commitments from work already in motion, checks in on Chat or each person's channel and when someone is stuck finds who can unblock them. Project leads get the operating picture without another board to babysit.

(~210 chars; within ~240 budget)

### Value pillars (3)
1. **Commitments that close** — Every promise has an owner, due date and trail. → C1, C2, C3. Proof: product screenshots / ribbons.
2. **Check-ins where people work** — Calm prompts in Chat, Telegram or WhatsApp (when connected). → C4, C5, C6, C7(Beta), C8. Proof: Chat UI.
3. **Unblocks with context** — Stuck work routes to the right person with judgment, then reports for leads. → C9, C10, C11, C12. Proof: escalation + reports.
